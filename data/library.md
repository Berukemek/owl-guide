# KSU Libraries and Study Rooms
Sources: https://campus.kennesaw.edu/offices-services/library/about/hours.php and https://kennesaw.edu/library/visit-the-library.php

## Locations
- Kennesaw Campus: Horace W. Sturgis Library (LB), 385 Cobb Ave NW, Kennesaw, GA 30144. Phone 470-578-6202. The KSU Service Desk (tech help) is on the 4th floor, Suite 4500.
- Marietta Campus: L.V. Johnson Library (Building C), 910 Hornet Loop, Marietta, GA 30060. Phone 470-578-7276.

## Fall 2026 hours (posted for University Libraries; Aug 23 – Dec 14)
- Mon–Thu 7:30 AM – 11:00 PM
- Fri 7:30 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sat 9:30 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sun 1:00 PM – 9:00 PM
These hours are posted for the University Libraries as a whole. Johnson Library (Marietta) may differ, so suggest calling 470-578-7276 to confirm.

## Exceptions (these override the regular hours)
- Sat–Sun Nov 21–22: Closed
- Mon–Wed Nov 23–25: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Thu–Sun Nov 26–29: Closed (Thanksgiving)
- Mon–Thu Dec 7–10 (finals): 7:30 AM – 12:00 AM (midnight)
- Winter break: Dec 15–18 8 AM–5 PM; Dec 19–20 closed; Dec 21–24 8 AM–5 PM; Dec 25 – Jan 3 closed; Jan 4–8 8 AM–5 PM; Jan 9 closed
- Spring 2027 hours start Sun Jan 10.

## Help
- Research consultations: Mon–Fri 9 AM – 5 PM
- Live chat with a librarian: 24/7

## Study rooms (reserve online)
Room availability changes every time someone books, so you can't see live availability. Always send students to the live booking page:
- Kennesaw Campus (Sturgis Library) study rooms: https://kennesaw.libcal.com/reserve/studyroomsK
- Marietta Campus (Johnson Library) study rooms: https://kennesaw.libcal.com/reserve/studyroomsM
Tip: the booking page shows open time slots in a grid. Pick a slot and sign in with your KSU account to confirm.
