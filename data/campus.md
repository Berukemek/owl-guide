# KSU Campuses, Buildings and Directions
Source: the official KSU printed campus maps (Kennesaw and Marietta). Interactive map: https://maps.kennesaw.edu/
Format: Name (abbreviation) [building number].
Directions use map orientation: north is the top of the map.

## Campus addresses
- Kennesaw Campus main entrance: 1000 Chastain Rd NW, Kennesaw, GA 30144
- Marietta Campus main entrance: 1100 South Marietta Pkwy SE, Marietta, GA 30060
- The two campuses are about 9 miles apart; Marietta is south of Kennesaw.
- Main phone: 470-KSU-INFO (470-578-4636)

## Commonly confused names
- "Academic Building" = Academic Building (H) [830] on the MARIETTA Campus. Don't confuse it with the "Academic Learning Center" (ALC) [480] on the Kennesaw Campus. If it's unclear, mention both.
- "Burruss" (often misspelled "Buruss" or "Burris") = Burruss Building (BB) [560], Kennesaw Campus.
- "The library" = Sturgis Library (Kennesaw) or Lawrence V. Johnson Library (Marietta).
- "The dining hall" = The Commons (Kennesaw) or Stingers (Marietta).
- "The Student Center" = Carmichael Student Center (Kennesaw) or Joe Mack Wilson Student Center (Marietta).
- "The rec center" = Siegel Student Recreation and Activities Center (Kennesaw) or Recreation and Wellness Center (Marietta).

## Approximate walking times (estimated from the campus maps at a normal pace; always say "about")
Kennesaw Campus:
- Between buildings around the Campus Green (Student Center, Burruss, Commons, ALC, Kennesaw Hall, Sturgis Library, University Hall): about 2–5 min
- Student Center → Science / Clendenin: about 5 min
- Student Center → Siegel Rec Center or Convocation Center: about 5 min
- Student Center → Arts area (Wilson, Bailey Performance Center, Zuckerman Museum): about 5–7 min
- Student Center → Social Sciences / Willingham Hall: about 5 min
- Student Center → The Summit (High Point Café): about 7–10 min
- Student Center → University Village: about 10 min; → KSU Place: about 15 min
- Student Center → Austin Residence Complex / main entrance on Chastain Rd: about 10–12 min
- Student Center → Town Point (Admissions): about 15 min
- Student Center → Fifth Third Bank Stadium or KSU Center (across I-75): about 20 min (a long walk; driving or the campus shuttle may be easier)
- North end (KSU Place) → south end (Chastain Rd): about 20–25 min
Marietta Campus (compact; most buildings are within 10 min of each other):
- Student Center → Mathematics / Administration / Johnson Library: about 2–4 min
- Johnson Library → Academic Building, Norton Hall, Crawford Lab: about 2–3 min
- Johnson Library → Atrium / Design buildings: about 4–5 min
- Student Center → Stingers Dining Hall: about 8–10 min
- Student Center → Recreation and Wellness Center: about 4–5 min
- Atrium → Architecture / Textile Center / K Field: about 3–5 min
- Student Center → Civil and Environmental Engineering or Wilder Communications: about 10–12 min
- Student Center → University Columns: about 12–15 min

# KENNESAW CAMPUS
Layout: I-75 runs along the east side. The main entrance on Chastain Rd is at the south end, by the Campus Entry Lawn. Student housing is at the north end (KSU Place, University Village) and the south end (Austin Residence Complex, The Summit). The academic core surrounds the Campus Green in the middle. The football stadium and KSU Center are across I-75 to the east.

## Central core (around the Campus Green)
- Campus Green: the big central lawn, a landmark for giving directions.
- Carmichael Student Center (ST) [395]: on the west side of the Campus Green. Inside: Chick-fil-A, Panda Express, Moe's, Hissho Sushi, Jamba, The Market, Talon Express, Card Services, Talon One Service Center, OwlTEC, Parking and Transportation. The KSU Bookstore is in the same complex, at the south end.
- Burruss Building (BB) [560]: on the north edge of the Campus Green, just south of The Commons and across the walkway from the Academic Learning Center. McAlister's Deli is inside.
- The Commons Dining Hall [540]: north of Burruss, near Prillaman Health Sciences and the Central Deck.
- Academic Learning Center (ALC) [480]: just north of the Student Center, across from Burruss. Cinnabon is inside.
- Bagwell Education Building (BEB) [580]: east of Burruss, near I-75.
- Kennesaw Hall (KH) [585]: on the east side of the Campus Green (administration).
- Prillaman Health Sciences (HS) [520]: north of The Commons, next to the Central Deck.
- Sturgis Library (LB) [385]: west of the Student Center, between Pilcher and University Hall.
- Pilcher Building (PS) [375]: just west of Sturgis Library.
- University Hall (UH) [430]: north of Sturgis Library.
- English Building (EB) [440]: north of University Hall.
- Willingham Hall (WH) [420]: west of University Hall. Student Activities office.
- Social Sciences Building (SO) [402]: on the west side, next to Willingham Hall. Starbucks is inside.
- Legacy Gazebo [410]: near the English Building and Social Sciences.
- Math and Statistics (MS) [365]: just south of Sturgis Library.
- Tech Annex [361]: south-west of the library, near Public Safety.
- Public Safety [351]: next to the West Deck.

# MARIETTA CAMPUS
Layout: South Marietta Pkwy runs along the north edge. The main entrance is at the north-east corner. The Student Center and large parking lots are at the north end, academic buildings fill the middle, housing is on the east and south-west sides, and K Field is in the south-center. Buildings are known by letter.

## North end
- Joe Mack Wilson Student Center (A) [860]: north-center, south of Lots P33 and P35. Inside: Chick-fil-A, Dunkin', The Local Spot, High Roller Sushi, Jamba, the Dance Theater, the KSU Bookstore, Card Services, Talon One Service Center, Parking and Transportation.
- Recreation and Wellness Center (S1) [955]: north-east, by Lot P27. The Marietta Health Clinic is inside.

## Middle of campus
- Mathematics Building (D) [850]: just south of the Student Center.
- Administration Building (B) [900]: south-east of the Mathematics Building.
- Lawrence V. Johnson Library (C) [910]: center of campus, south of the Administration Building.
- Norton Hall (R2) [920]: just east of the library.
- Crawford Lab Building (E) [846]: west of the library.
- Science Lab Annex [846] and Engineering Lab Building (G) [834]: west of the Academic Building.
- Engineering Technology Center (Q) [840]: on the west side, next to Lot P30.
- Academic Building (H) [830]: just south of the library.
- Design 1 (I1) [824] and Design 2 (I2) [820]: south of the Engineering Lab.
- Atrium Building (J) [680]: south-center, east of the Design buildings. STACKS and Starbucks are inside.
- Howell Hall: housing, east of Norton Hall. The Housing and Residence Life Office is nearby.

## South side
- Stingers Dining Hall (X) [806]: south-west, next to Deck P60 and Hornet Village.
- Hornet Village: housing beside Stingers.
- K Field: the big field in the south-center. The Nobi Cha food truck parks here.
- W. Clair Harris Textile Center (M) [650]: east of K Field.
- Architecture (N) [640]: south-east of the Textile Center.
- Civil and Environmental Engineering (L) [655]: south of K Field, past Polytechnic Lane.
- Student Competition Team Building (O) [659]: south, near Lot P22.
- Wilder Communications Center (T) [643]: far south.
- Gymnasium (S2) [635]: south-east, by Lot P21.
- S. Walter Kelly Sr. Memorial Field: south-east.

## Housing (Marietta)
- University Courtyard (buildings 1000–3000): east side.
- University Commons (buildings 100–600): south-east.
- University Columns: far south-west.
- Hornet Village and Howell Hall: see above.

## Marietta parking
- Big lots at the north end: P31, P33, P35 (central parking) and P27, P38 (east parking, by the Rec Center).
- Deck P60: south-west, next to Stingers.
- Lot P22: south, near the Student Competition Team Building.
