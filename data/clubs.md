# KSU Clubs and Involvement
Sources: https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/ and KSU college pages.
KSU has 300+ Registered Student Organizations (RSOs). The full list is on Owl Life (https://owllife.kennesaw.edu, log in with your NetID). Owl Life is also where you join clubs and find student events.
Student Activities: Willingham Hall, (470) 578-6275 (Kennesaw); Room 230 Wilson Student Center, (470) 578-2716 (Marietta); studentactivities@kennesaw.edu

## Ways to get involved
- Fraternity and Sorority Life: 33 fraternities and sororities (councils: IFC, Panhellenic, NPHC, MGC). https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/fraternity-sorority-life/index.php
- Esports at KSU: competitive teams, events and a social community. https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/wellbeing/sportsrec/programs/esports.php
- Scrappy's Bucket List: 63 activities to complete before graduation for rewards. https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/scrappys-bucket-list.php
- Student Government Association (SGA): student advocacy. https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/student-government-association.php
- Student Leadership programs and workshops. https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/student-leadership/index.php
- KSU Student Media: newspaper, radio and magazine. https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/student-media/index.php
- Student Volunteerism and Service. https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/student-affairs/involvement/student-volunteerism/index.php

## Tech and computing clubs (College of Computing and Software Engineering)
- Analytics and Data Science Organization (ADSO): guest lectures, software demos, workshops, networking, research support
- Artificial Intelligence Club (AIClub): self-paced learning in reinforcement learning, time series, NLP and more
- Association of Information Technology Professionals (AITP): IT career development and industry networking
- Game Design and Development Club (GDDC): programmers, artists, musicians and designers making games together
- Girls Who Code Campus Loop (GWC): events and workshops supporting women in computing
- IEEE Computer Society (IEEE-CS): computing events, research and professional growth
- International Student Association (ISA): celebrates international diversity and cultural awareness
- International Student Foundation (ISF): support for international students adjusting to the U.S.
- Kennesaw Society of Black Engineers (KSBE): NSBE chapter with study halls and professional development
- OWL Cyber Club (CYBER): cybersecurity workshops and competitions for all skill levels
- Owl Research Hive (ORH): helps students get into research and present their work
- Robotics & Automation Society (RAS): robotics projects, conferences and workshops (IEEE affiliated)
- Software Acceleration Team (SwAT): team software projects, workshops and industry connections
- Upsilon Pi Epsilon (UPE): computing honor society
- Women in Cybersecurity (WiCys): networking and mentoring for women in cyber
- Women in Technology – Campus (WIT): STEAM career exploration, mentoring and scholarships

- Model NATO and Model United Nations: debate conferences
- National Association of Home Builders student chapter
- Night Owl Racing: modified production vehicles on a budget
- Owl Robotics Competition Team: VEX and combat robotics
- Solar Vehicle Team
- Steel Bridge Competition Team
