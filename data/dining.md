# KSU Dining – Fall 2026
Source: https://campus.kennesaw.edu/offices-services/dining/hours-locations.php
Menus: Nutrislice app. Mobile ordering: Grubhub app (pay with Dining Dollars, KCash or card).
Meal plans work at: The Commons, Stingers, High Point Café, On the Fly (Kennesaw) and Revolution Pizza Express (Marietta). Dining Dollars work at any university-operated dining location.
University Dining Office: Mon–Fri 8:30 AM–4:30 PM, 470-578-2902 (Kennesaw), 470-578-4975 (Marietta).
