# KSU Academic Calendar (Fall 2026 and Spring 2027)
Source: https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/registrar/academic-calendars
Most classes are 14-week (full semester). 7-week sessions have their own dates, listed in brackets.
If a student doesn't say which session, give the 14-week date and mention that 7-week classes differ.

## Fall 2026
- Registration: opened 8:00 AM Mar 19; regular registration closed Aug 19 at 11:45 PM (the online calendar lists the drop/add end, Aug 28)
- First day of classes: Mon Aug 24 [7-Week II: Tue Oct 13]
- Drop/add: Mon Aug 24 – Fri Aug 28 [7-Week II also Sep 29 – Oct 14]
- Payment deadline: Fri Aug 28
- Labor Day (no classes): Mon Sep 7
- Midterm grades due: Fri Oct 30
- Last day to withdraw with a W: Fri Nov 6, 11:45 PM [7-Week I: Sep 23; 7-Week II: Nov 11]
- FALL BREAK / THANKSGIVING BREAK (no classes): Mon Nov 23 – Sun Nov 29. KSU's fall break is Thanksgiving week; there is no separate October fall break for 14-week classes.
- Last day of classes: Mon Dec 7 [7-Week I: Oct 7; 7-Week II: Wed Dec 2]
- Final exams: Tue Dec 8 – Mon Dec 14 [7-Week I: Oct 8–9; 7-Week II: Dec 3–4]
- Fall 2026 final exam schedule (exam time for each class): https://campus.kennesaw.edu/current-students/registrar/docs/fall-2026-final-exam-schedule.pdf
- Commencement: Dec 15 – Dec 19
- Final grades due: Thu Dec 17 at noon

## Spring 2027
- Registration: opened 8:00 AM Thu Oct 8, 2026; LAST DAY TO REGISTER is Fri Jan 15, 2027 at 11:45 PM
- First day of classes: Mon Jan 11 [7-Week II: Mon Mar 8]
- Drop/add: Mon Jan 11 – Fri Jan 15
- Payment deadline: Fri Jan 15 at 4:00 PM
- MLK Day (no classes): Mon Jan 18
- Graduation application deadline: Sat Jan 24 at 11:45 PM
- Spring break: Mon Mar 1 – Sun Mar 7
- Midterm grades due: Fri Mar 26
- Last day to withdraw with a W: Fri Apr 2, 11:45 PM [7-Week I: Feb 10; 7-Week II: Apr 6]
- Last day of classes: Mon Apr 26 [7-Week I: Feb 24; 7-Week II: Apr 20]
- Final exams: Tue Apr 27 – Mon May 3 [7-Week I: Feb 25–26]
- Commencement: May 4 – May 8
- Final grades due: Thu May 6 at noon

## Answering "when is…" questions
Use today's date to pick the right semester. Before Dec 15, 2026, "final exams" and "last day of classes" mean Fall 2026; "last day to register" means Spring 2027 registration, since Fall registration has already closed. Mention the other semester briefly if it helps.
